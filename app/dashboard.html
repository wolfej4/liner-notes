Every year of your Spotify listening, in one place

Liner Notes reads the history files from your Spotify data export and turns them into charts. The files are read inside this browser and never sent anywhere.

  1. On Spotify's Privacy settings page, request Extended streaming history. That's the one with every play since you opened your account.
  2. Wait for the email with your download link. Spotify quotes up to 30 days; it's often much faster.
  3. Drop the zip here, or the Streaming_History_Audio JSON files from inside it.
